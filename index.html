FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. The calculators are free, and you don't need to sign in. Trading needs an Amaltash account.

Where does the data come from?

Prices for 12,000+ US stocks and funds, updated nightly. US returns since 1928 from NYU Stern. Net worth by age from the Federal Reserve 2022 survey.

Which AI apps does it work in?

Claude today, on the web, desktop and in Claude Code. ChatGPT and Google once they approve it.

Does it see my personal data?

No. It only gets the numbers in your question. It does not need an account, and it cannot trade or move money.
